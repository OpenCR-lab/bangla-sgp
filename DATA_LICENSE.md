# Dataset licence — CC BY-4.0

The **Bangla-SGP dataset** (all files under `data/`: the human-annotated
sentence–gloss pairs and the synthetically generated pairs) is released under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY-4.0)**.

This matches the dataset availability statement in the paper:

> The Bangla-SGP dataset will be made publicly available upon publication
> through our official repository at GitHub. The dataset will be released
> under the CC BY-4.0 license and all augmentation rules, and documentation
> will be included in the repository.
> — *Dataset Availability Statement, LREC 2026, p. 10464*

## What you may do

- **Share** — copy and redistribute the dataset in any medium or format for any
  purpose, including commercial use.
- **Adapt** — remix, transform, and build upon the dataset for any purpose,
  including commercial use.

## What you must do

- **Attribution.** You must give appropriate credit, provide a link to the
  licence, and indicate if changes were made. A suitable attribution is the
  citation in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) / the "How to cite" section of the
  README. Cite the paper, name the dataset, and link this repository.
- **Indicate changes.** If you correct, filter, extend, or re-annotate rows,
  state that you did. Use the stable row IDs (`bsgp-*`) to point to the rows
  you changed or reproduced — please do not silently overwrite shared IDs.
